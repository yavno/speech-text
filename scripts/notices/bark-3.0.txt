Underdog Bark 3.0 and Bark 3.0 Clean — local speech models, downloaded when chosen
Speech recognition weights downloaded from Settings when the user chooses them, not
shipped in the app: Underdog Bark 3.0 (https://huggingface.co/ConwayResearch/Underdog-Bark-3.0)
and Underdog Bark 3.0 Clean (https://huggingface.co/ConwayResearch/Underdog-Bark-3.0-Clean)
by Conway Research, converted to Core ML by Fluid Inference
(https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/bark-3.0-coreml, revision
73b0c0da665d29ea0332e9f0d32da8e26a4cdb38). Published under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC-BY-4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Both models derive from:

  nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3, (c) NVIDIA Corporation, CC-BY-4.0
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
  moondream/parakeet-ultra, (c) Moondream, CC-BY-4.0
    https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra

Changes, as the upstream NOTICE states them: post-trained and compressed by Conway
Research. The FastConformer encoder architecture, TDT decoder, tokenizer and
feature-extractor configuration derive from the models above and remain under
CC-BY-4.0. Fluid Inference's Core ML conversion re-encodes the encoder's linear layers
as Core ML palettes, bit-exact to the upstream weights; the other tensors are the
upstream values at fp16. SpeechText runs the files unmodified, and removes the "<unk>"
token Bark writes for a quotation mark from the transcript.
